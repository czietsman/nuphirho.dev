Honoured, and genuinely humbled, to have received the Disruptor Award at CyberSentriq's NOVA Awards this quarter.

I believe the only way to lead is to serve. We have grown a great deal, and that growth is carried by people putting in long hours, hard work, and full dedication to make CyberSentriq better every day.

One line from my nomination stayed with me: "He always pushes boundaries by asking why, why not?" I have seen first-hand how AI can help me, and push me to be better, and I want that same opportunity for everyone else, at CyberSentriq and in the wider community too.

I'm happy to carry the momentum from the other winners' posts. It puts a spotlight on CyberSentriq and everyone who works here, and they deserve that more than I do.

Congratulations to my fellow winners, Amanda Cruz, Eddie Monaghan, Jean Camargo and Duane Meyer, and thank you to everyone who took the time to nominate a colleague.
